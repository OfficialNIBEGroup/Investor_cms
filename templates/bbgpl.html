ABOUT US

Welcome to Bharat Bio Gas

At Bharat Bio Gas, we believe every agricultural residue has the potential to become a valuable source of clean energy. Bharat Bio Gas is developing advanced Compressed Biogas (CBG) infrastructure that converts agricultural biomass into clean, renewable fuel and high-value organic bio-fertilizers.

Our integrated CBG project combines advanced anaerobic digestion, modern gas purification systems and intelligent process engineering to convert biomass into high-quality compressed biogas that meets national fuel quality standards.

Supporting India's SATAT Initiative

Bharat Bio Gas aligns with the SATAT (Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation) initiative launched by the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG) to promote Compressed Bio Gas (CBG) as a clean and sustainable transportation fuel.

Driving India's Renewable Energy Transition

Bharat Bio Gas is committed to developing integrated renewable energy infrastructure that converts biomass and municipal waste into clean energy solutions.

OUR TECHNOLOGIES

From Biomass To CBG

1

Anaerobic Digestion

Converts agricultural biomass into biogas through a natural biological process.

2

Biogas Upgradation

Removes impurities from raw biogas to produce high-quality CBG.

3

Gas Purification

Removes moisture, CO₂ and H₂S to ensure clean and efficient fuel.

4

CBG Compression

Compresses purified CBG for safe storage and easy transportation.

5

Organic Bio-Fertilizer Recovery

Converts the remaining digestate into nutrient-rich organic bio-fertilizer.

OUR PRODUCTS

Sustainable Products For A Cleaner Tomorrow

Bharat Bio Gas develops renewable energy products that maximize resource efficiency while supporting India's transition towards a low-carbon and circular economy.

Compressed Biogas (CBG)

Clean. Renewable. Reliable.

Bharat Bio Gas produces upgraded biogas through the anaerobic digestion of agricultural biomass. The gas is purified to achieve a methane content of ≥99% and complies with IS 16087:2025 quality standards.

Key Features
  • Renewable alternative to CNG
  • Methane Purity ≥99%
  • High Calorific Value
  • Low Carbon Emissions
  • IS 16087:2025 Compliant
Applications
  • City Gas Distribution (CGD)
  • Automotive Fuel
  • Industrial Heating
  • Boilers & Furnaces
  • Power Generation

Solid Fermented Organic Manure (FOM)

Restoring Soil Naturally

Solid Fermented Organic Manure is produced from nutrient-rich digestate generated during the CBG production process.

Liquid Fermented Organic Manure (LFOM)

Natural Nutrition For Better Crop Growth

Liquid Fermented Organic Manure is a nutrient-rich liquid bio-fertilizer recovered during digestate processing.

Elemental Sulfur

High-Purity Sulfur Recovery

During the gas purification process, sulfur compounds are safely removed from biogas. The recovered elemental sulfur can be utilized for industrial applications.
