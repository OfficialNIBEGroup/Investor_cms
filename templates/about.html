The NIBE Story

Our innovation and technology in defence sector is aimed to strengthen strategic self reliance under the flagship Atmanirbhar Bharat Initiative of honourable Prime Minister.

Building India’s Defence Future

We design and deliver integrated defence solutions that power India's strategic autonomy. From advanced artillery systems and smart electronics to indigenous small arms and aerospace systems, NIBE Limited partners with the nation to engineer strength, scale, and self-reliance.

At NIBE, we don't just manufacture defence systems, we build sovereign capability. Every product, every project, is a step towards a self-reliant and secure India.
